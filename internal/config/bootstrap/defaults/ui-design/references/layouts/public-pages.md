# Explain a specific product and a useful next action

Use for a public explanation or acquisition surface. A repeated-work application has another job. Let supplied truth and material determine the opening, evidence and continuation rather than reproducing a fixed marketing sequence.

## Establish the subject

Make product identity, what it does, who benefits and an appropriate next action understandable. A genuine product view, useful demonstration, image of the actual subject or strong specific type can lead. Choose the composition from that material.

| Opening | When it helps | Cost to examine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product-led | Behavior or artifact is the strongest explanation | The demonstration must be legible and truthful |
| Split | Explanation and useful visual support one decision | Both halves need balanced mass and coherent reading order |
| Type-led | Words/facts carry the subject | Avoid a generic enormous promise with little substance |
| Media/full-bleed | Appearance or context earns scale | Cap text measure and check real-image contrast |
| Asymmetric/editorial | Importance and reading sequence are unequal | Preserve orientation and a clear action |

The first relevant viewport should establish the subject and a useful next step. Test actual width and height; there is no universal fold size or reason to compress every feature into it.

## Build only earned continuation

Explain capabilities, workflow or adoption questions through meaningful comparison, demonstration, content groups or an argument. Necessary roles can merge on a short page. A thin brief does not require a large site.

Evidence, use cases, pricing, integrations and FAQ are conditional on actual material and decision needs. Keep supplied claims verifiable and illustrative data clearly illustrative. Do not create a logo wall, invented metric, countdown, security claim or fake customer quote.

A real action leads to the supported destination or honestly labeled prototype boundary. Necessary support/policy destinations follow the product. Do not end every page with a mailing list or repeated CTA band by habit.

## Give the direction substance

Choose typography, scale, rhythm, image contribution, geometry and color together. Editorial does not mandate serif; minimal does not mean empty; corporate does not mean blue. Several accents can help a real hierarchy without decorating every block. A requested material deserves a coherent implementation, not removal to satisfy a generic warning.

Use cards for independent items, weighted grids for unequal importance and ordinary prose when that explains best. Avoid a wrapper for every paragraph or a polished fake dashboard standing in for product evidence.

## Deliver and verify

Authored navigation and copy appear directly. Reserve real image geometry and use a matching derivative where Blur-up is appropriate. Prioritize critical opening media; a loading effect cannot compensate for deferred useful content. Apply [loading](../loading-and-progress.md), a justified [disclosure](../components/accordion.md) or a real [form](../components/forms.md) only where needed.

Inspect the actual next action, long Korean headings, missing media, keyboard access, submission/recovery and relevant resized/zoomed conditions. A design reference shows one possible composition, not proof of brand truth, user preference or production behavior.
