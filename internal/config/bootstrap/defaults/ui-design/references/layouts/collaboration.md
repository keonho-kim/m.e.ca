# Compose around shared work and usable results

Choose the organizing axis: state, a case/conversation, an input/result or actual activity. Collaboration and AI do not require a board, chat and dashboard on the same screen.

## Boards and queues

A board helps when stage movement is the task. Rows help when people compare fields or process a queue repeatedly. Keep scope, useful view/query controls, identifiable items and a clear inspection/action path. Assignment, workload and automation are conditional features.

Make count and selection meanings consistent across alternate views. A view switch does not create another business object or data model. Stable compact controls suit repeated operation; do not replay a reveal on every filter or move ordinary actions into an unnecessary modal.

## Conversation and support

Give the queue/list, selected history, composer/actions and relevant identity enough space. A three-pane desktop can reduce recall, but each pane must answer a real question. Recompose secondary context before crushing the central conversation.

Keep the input/draft stable while reading history or inspecting another object. Distinguish current responsibility, send state and actual case outcome. Knowledge suggestions, templates or SLA belong when they are useful supported facts, not filler.

## AI and grounded work

Show task/input scope, a useful result representation and truthful execution/recovery. Conversation is optional. A document, slide, data table, code preview or visual artifact may be the main workspace. The generated artifact and its structure can lead while tools remain compact.

Decide where sources and evidence belong from frequency and comparison need. Persistent sources improve visibility but consume width; disclosure can simplify the frame but adds consultation cost. Citations should remain recognizable and reveal the actual relevant record without stealing unrelated context.

Separate conversation, task observation and artifact state. A stream is not proof of a completed document, and a disconnected observer does not prove work stopped. Keep real input and output readable instead of fabricating thoughts, logs or progress for atmosphere.

## Authored content and local tools

An outline, schema tree or filmstrip navigates task objects. It can earn a persistent pane without implying another global sidebar. Parallel outputs should be easy to distinguish; avoid a wizard that makes a document look like a prerequisite for its presentation.

Use an inspector for relevant suggestions, source detail or composition controls. Preserve sufficient central measure and make the tool/content hierarchy clear through proportion and typography before adding material.

## Activity and feedback

Include activity when it explains actual changes, responsibility or history. Keep planned, pending and confirmed events distinct. Repeated live updates should preserve reading position and avoid flooding toasts or announcements.

Use local initial skeletons for genuinely fetched structures; retain safe drafts and reveal real streams as they arrive. Select [loading/progress](../loading-and-progress.md), [notifications](../components/notifications.md) and [actions/status](../components/actions-and-status.md) only for the supported task.

Check selection during loading, failed send, reconnect, changed scope, return to ongoing work and long content. Native/established controls remain familiar under expressive material. False collaborators, simulated liveness and a composer that invents success are design defects, regardless of visual polish.
