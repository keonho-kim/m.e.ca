# Learning, lessons and quizzes

Use this when people work through material, submit an attempt and return to feedback or progress. Choose the conditions for completion and the identities that preserve its evidence.

[Do & Don’t](do-and-dont.md) · [Implementation Q&A](implementation-qa.md)

## Main flow

1. Open the course and show available material according to enrollment and prerequisites. Resume at a meaningful recorded position.
2. Start a quiz or assignment under a known version and attempt policy. Save responses as allowed while preserving the actual deadline and eligibility.
3. Submit or end an attempt according to the defined rules. Show accepted, awaiting grading, graded and recoverable failure states.
4. Report progress and feedback from the correct attempts. Keep reattempts, manual grading changes and content revisions intelligible.

## Implementation boundaries

- Bind an attempt to its learner, course version, questions or rubric, and eligibility. Keep accepted answers and grades understandable after content edits.
- Define what counts as completion, passing, and a remaining attempt. Distinguish saved draft answers, final submission and published grade.
- Use an authoritative time policy when deadlines or time limits matter. State what happens when the connection drops or time expires during a submit.
- Scope learner, instructor and reviewer access to responses, answer keys, comments and individual or aggregate grades.

## Acceptance cases

- An attempt submit is retried across its deadline. Show the accepted outcome associated with that attempt.
- A question or rubric changes. A previous submission and grade retain their meaning.
- Publish a manually scored attempt and verify learner visibility and the corresponding progress record.
