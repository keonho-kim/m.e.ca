# Customer support Do & Don’t

| Decision | Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reply visibility | Label the audience before sending and carry it through attachments, search, exports and delivery. | Treat a staff-only note as public because it shares the ticket's composer. |
| Intake | Record stable inbound identity and verify which ticket and requester it belongs to. | Merge requests solely by a similar title or unverified sender text. |
| Assignment | Validate eligibility and handle concurrent reassignment with a visible result. | Show the queue operator a previous assignee as confirmed after a conflict. |
| Send failure | Preserve the draft or accepted reply and identify whether sending can be retried safely. | Resend a customer-facing message and close the ticket on every timeout. |
| Resolution | Use an explicit status transition and provide a usable route for the permitted follow-up. | Infer resolution from a sent message or a read receipt. |
| Metrics | Use an agreed clock and define pauses, owner and eligible states when reporting service deadlines. | Present an unsupported deadline as a guarantee. |
