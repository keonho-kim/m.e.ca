# Social feed Do & Don’t

| Decision | Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Show the audience before publishing and enforce it on the final read or delivery path. | Treat a list filtered by the browser as an access boundary. |
| Shares and quotes | Re-evaluate referenced content when displaying it and define what survives removal. | Widen visibility by copying restricted content into a public share. |
| Relationships | State which routes and notifications a mute, unfollow or block affects. | Make all relationship changes behave like the same hidden row. |
| Paging and updates | Keep a stable identity and sort tie breaker. Reveal new entries without resetting the current reading position. | Replace the entire feed on every reaction or late page. |
| Reactions | Scope the person's selection and its retry to the actual post. Reconcile counts with confirmed results. | Report speculative counts as settled after a failed update. |
| Derived views | Recheck canonical visibility at delivery or enforce its current revision on materialized views. | Keep a previous audience indefinitely in search, cache or previews. |
