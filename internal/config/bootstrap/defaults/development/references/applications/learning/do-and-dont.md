# Learning Do & Don’t

| Decision | Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content versions | Keep the version and relevant answers or rubric for historical results. | Regrade old responses against changed questions without an explicit policy. |
| Progress | Use the agreed evidence for completion and show applicable prerequisites. | Treat opening a lesson or buffering video as course completion. |
| Attempts | Carry one attempt identity through autosave, resume and submit. | Count repeated transport requests as repeat attempts. |
| Timing | State the deadline, grace and expiry outcome where timing applies. | Decide whether an answer arrived on time from the user's local clock alone. |
| Feedback | Distinguish accepted, awaiting grade, graded and published feedback. | Reveal answer keys or another learner's grade through generic result access. |
| Retry | Announce what was saved and offer the allowed next action. | Clear a recoverable attempt when final submission encounters a network error. |
