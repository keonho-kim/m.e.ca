# Learning implementation Q&A

## What happens when a lesson changes after completion?

Choose whether existing completion applies to the new version or a new requirement is introduced. Preserve the old evidence and show what remains required. Reuse the original question or rubric version when interpreting historical work.

## How do autosave and final submission fit together?

Store draft progress according to the attempt policy. Finalization pins the accepted response or an equivalent version and produces its own acknowledgement. A late autosave respects the finalized attempt state. Retry the same finalization identity after a lost response.

## What time determines a timed attempt's result?

Use an agreed start, deadline and authoritative acceptance rule. Decide whether arrival, accepted processing or another event satisfies it. Exercise a slow request around the cutoff and a response lost after an accepted submit.

## How are several grades reduced to a single result?

Define whether latest, highest, average or another rule applies. Determine which attempts are eligible and when a pending grade contributes. Preserve individual results and show the meaning of the aggregate to the learner or reviewer.
